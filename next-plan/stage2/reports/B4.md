## Summary
The Threader and its arena, the Manifold, run in IceHiveCore on B1's boss framework. The fight is a deterministic simulation with a state API for B5's renderer.

The Manifold mission is two pumps → BOSS the Threader → EXTRACT. It has its own calibration preset, `bosses`, gated in its own CI step. All three bands pass:
- **Veteran:** the boss is reached in 100 % of runs and killed in 90.6 % of those; kill median 72.3 s.
- **Nightmare:** 15.6 % wins.
- **Recruit:** 100 % wins.

The classic calibration and every other preset are byte-identical to native-metal. I compared md5s locally after every step and in CI. Since the merge, the missions preset matches M1's new baseline, ea6b8aaa….

What was built:
- **The arena** (`Manifold.swift`), as layout data:
  - a 14 × 14 m island with a 4 × 4 m pump housing (cover), the catwalk ring at 13–17 m, four bridges, and the abyss below;
  - four pipe trunks at 5 m forming a «#» and the ring duct at 8 m, all R2 overhead routes, plus eight riser pipes and four duct mouths;
  - four R6 steam valves, the approach (landing pad, walkway, two pump consoles) and the lift.
  - `LevelLayout.manifold` carries every piece for B5 and B2's kit. The walk grid and the routes change at run time and are restored for the next sortie.
- **The boss** (`GameThreader.swift`):
  - `Difficulty.threader` rows: HP 3200 / 4500 / 7500 × hpMul, as in the design;
  - a chain body: the head, 18 segments and 5 lantern nodes;
  - every design move: lure and strike, shed, climb, coil, rime spit, squeeze, pull-down, deck chase;
  - three phases, damage routed to one bar, and class counterplay.
- **The squad** (`GameThreaderAI.swift`): targets, dodges, abilities and positions for the bots, the autopilot and the turret. This closes the four open issues B1 left me:
  - its parts no longer count as a crowd for abilities;
  - targeting priority is the head while it is down, then the exposed nodes; the turret takes the nodes;
  - marines collide with its low parts;
  - nothing burns on its frost-plated parts.
- **Tests:** 25 new core tests in ThreaderTests, plus four existing tests updated to count the Manifold. CI ran 274 tests with 0 failures.

**Tuning.** With the design's numbers the squad (about 470 raw dps) took up to 35 % of the boss's health in one 2 s window, and the Veteran kill median was 29–36 s. To reach 60–100 s without touching the design's multipliers, I added these rules in the Threader's own file:
- **Pain:** taking 20 % of max HP in one exposure ends it. The head pulls up at once, a coil lets go, and the ice on it breaks.
- **Armour on the trunks:** the head takes ×0.25 up there. Nodes up there in P1–P2 are behind frost plates (×1/6, so ×0.25 in effect).
- **Lock pauses:** no lure lock for 6 s after its arrival, 16 s after a strike, or 6 s after a phase change.
- **Climb and retries:** the first climb comes at 14 s. A move that can't start retries in 0.5 s instead of waiting out its cooldown.
- **P3 keeps away:** it slithers to the ring for 4 s and charges from there.
- **Arena stream:** × 0.45 / 0.8 / 1.6 by tier.

## Handoffs → B5
- **Body:** B1's `bossPartCount` (24 parts), `bossPart(k)` and `bossBodyPoint(behind:)`. Segments follow the head's path by arc length (2 m, then every 1.6 m). Heights: 5 m on the trunks, up to 8 m in the duct, head down at deck + 0.5 m, coil ring at 2 m, P3 at deck + 0.6 m.
- **Node states:**
  - plated = `bossPart(k).up && boss.phase < 2`;
  - stripped = from the `.plateStrip` cue;
  - popped = `bossPart(k).popped`, with `.bossPartPopped` and `.scream`.
- **The lure** (`game.threader`, a `ThreaderView`): `lantern`, `lureSpot`, `lureRadius`, `locked` (draw the beam hot white while ≥ 0) and per-marine `exposure` (it locks at 0.8 s).
- **Other state:** the strike, the coil (with its gap), the squeeze bursts (`threaderBurst(k)`), the pull-down and `fallenTrunk`, `shedWarn`, `inDuct`.
- **Telegraphs:**
  - strike: `.drop`, 4.35 → 3 m over 1.2 s (0.6 s in P3), frost-white;
  - coil: a `.ring` of 3.5 m for 1.5 s, then a `.drop` closing 3.5 → 1.2 m over 3.4 s, orange;
  - rime: a `.beam` 35 × 2.6 m, 1 s wind-up, red;
  - squeeze: per burst, a `.steam` lane plus an amber gauge `.ring`, 1.5 s warning then 3 s of steam;
  - pull-down: a `.lane` 35 × 3.2 m, 2 s, red;
  - charge: a `.cone` 12 m wide 0.3 rad, 1 s, frost-white.
- **Events and audio:** B1's boss events plus 25 `.bossCue` cues, the four radio toasts, and camera shakes at the arrival, the crush and the pull crash. B5 maps the audio from the cues.
- **B2's kit:** every arena piece and its coordinates are in `Manifold.arena`; the route graph is `Manifold.overhead`. The full list is in the report.

## Open issues
1. **Manifold AFK is reported, not gated.** An idle player on the landing pad is downed late or never, because the bots hold the walkway. AFK isn't in B4's bands.
2. **Veteran wins 90.6 %**, above the classic 70–85 % band (reported, not gated). Raising the Veteran stream to ×1.0 brings wins to 82.8 %, but «killed» drops to 82.8 %, only 3 points above its 80 % gate.
3. **The tuning rules above deviate from the design's numbers:** head armour on the trunks, plates, pain, lock pauses, the early first climb, P3 keep-away. Without them a single window could take a whole phase.
4. **The lure locks a marine standing still mostly while hunting** (after 9 s with no lock). This is by design.
5. **Nightmare is harsh:** 15.6 % wins, against 22 % on the classic mission.
6. **Charges are rare** (0.9 per Veteran fight) and strikes rarely hit (0.4 per fight).
7. **The coil's centre is clamped to stay over the island**, so it may not sit exactly on the marine it targets.
8. **No allocation-count test.**
9. **Local app typecheck:** the same 26 stub-gap errors as base a047ef5, none from this package; CI's build is clean.
10. **Repository size:** GitHub warned «Repository is approaching its size quota» on the push to native-metal. This package adds no binaries.
