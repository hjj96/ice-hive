This is a functional fix, not a restyle: same fonts, colours, panels and voice, no new text, no core change. Calibration is identical by construction.

- **Tips come one at a time.** A queue (`UI/ToastQueue.swift`) sorts every toast into three lanes:
  - **Threats** show at once: «{call-sign} pinned», «… down», «You're down», and since the B4 merge «Coil — get out!» and «It's got a light — stay out of it!».
  - **First-sighting tips** (`toast.tip.*`) show one at a time, in arrival order, 0.8 s apart. They never start under a banner, a menu, or while «Pinned — dash!» / «You're down» is on the hint line. They show only while the thing they name is there (`UI/ToastThing.swift`).
  - **Barks** (upgrade names, «Lights up», …) show at once, one at a time. One that waited 3 s is dropped, because the screen already shows what it reports.
- **A tip whose moment has passed** leaves the line when its turn comes. It takes its old place again when its thing returns (the next rattle, the next perch). Nothing is thrown away: a tip stays unseen only if its thing never comes back that mission.
- **The toast column is shortened and has a mirrored twin.**
  - At most two rows (a tip and a bark). The new left column sits under the stats panel.
  - Each new toast takes the side where it hides least: the thing it names, the objective marker, the squad, the boss, the lift deck.
  - On a tie it goes away from where the marker points. In the first minute that is the top right, over the bridge and its overhead.
- **The objective marker never sits on toast text.** Inside a toast it stops where its ray enters it.
- **Banners never sit on toasts.** Under a banner nothing new starts, and a column it covers steps aside.
- **The hold.**
  - In a hold or boss fight, a banner takes the middle, high or low slot that hides the fewest marines, the boss and the least deck. On a phone that is the low slot.
  - A calm hint steps aside under a banner, so «DIG IN» and the lift-pulse hint are read one after the other.
  - The pulse hint stays at the bottom, where it covers 0 % of the deck.
