- **Why 34 ms.** The app has two frame slots on one in-order queue. A buffer that takes ≥ 34 ms holds both slots through a tick, and every frame queued behind it waits too.
- **Seconds under 34 ms are clean.** Of 1,463 such seconds, 7 drew fewer than 56 frames, caused by compositor drawable waits or main-thread host stalls.

**The cold wait split into its parts** (from the app's own line):

- The old formula subtracted the compiler start-up from the whole wait, so its 0.5 s budget also had to absorb the world build, which every launch pays and which grows with a slow host.
- Example, aa1d1ad's cold re-run at 0.60 s: 0.05 s of preload tail beyond the start-up, plus a 0.55 s build, while warm launches on the same runner built in 0.32 s.

## Rules before and after

**GPU drawn (swarm3x, release-combat).**
- **Before:** drawnAvg ≥ min(55, calm − 2). Failing that, a pass if ≥ 4 seconds without "host-GPU-stall" (> 100 ms buffer, > 10× GPU span) averaged ≥ that floor.
- **After:**
  - drawnAvg ≥ 55 passes, as before.
  - Otherwise each second is classed **host-held** (slowest buffer ≥ 34 ms and ≥ 10× the window's median GPU time per frame, the gate's `gpu ema`), **the loop's** (loop tick > 43 ms), or **host-free** (the rest).
  - Host-free seconds must average ≥ 55; after an in-part re-run they are pooled with the first run's.
  - With none left, the row reads «gpu drawn not measurable: the host held a command buffer >= 34 ms in N of M s» and passes.
  - The calm floor is gone; calm stays as the reference in the GPU line.

**Cold first launch.**
- **Before:** renderer wait = `metal world ready` − min(wait, first request − 0.3 s) ≤ 0.5 s; worst tick ≤ 150 ms; cold load ≤ warm median + 2.5 s; after a failure, a cold re-run and the better of two.
- **After:**
  - renderer wait = max(0, old value − the part's warm-launch median world build) ≤ 0.5 s. It is never larger than the old value.
  - New tripwire: the warm median world build ≤ 0.75 s (recorded per-part maximum 0.46 s).
  - Everything else is unchanged.

**Every other check is unchanged.**

## Handoffs: what a package does when a frame-gate row fails now

1. **«gpu drawn avg X < 55.0: Y/s in the N s the host left alone (M of K s held >= 34 ms)»** is no longer host noise.
   - Look at the slots and drawables (`skip=`, `nodraw=`, `drw=` in the `hb` lines) and at the GPU cost.
   - Compare with native-metal's latest run. A re-push will not make it pass.
2. **«gpu drawn not measurable: …»** is a pass; nothing to do.
3. **«cold launch: the scene waited X s for the Metal preload beyond the compiler start-up and a warm launch's world build (> 0.5)»** means both cold launches waited longer than this runner's warm launches.
   - Check what your change adds to the preload: `pipelines built …`, `meshes … in N ms`, `kit …`.
   - Or to the first launch's world build: `squad: … maps ASTC`, `station:`, `swarm: creatures`.
4. **«warm launches: the world build after the Metal preload took a median X s (> 0.75)»** means every launch's MetalWorld build grew (recorded 0.16–0.46 s).
5. **Loop-side rows and the cold load tripwire are unchanged** (one in-part re-run as before).
6. **calm** no longer needs to share a part with swarm3x / release-combat. **firstlaunch** stays first in a part that has warm demos (part a).
7. **B2, B3, V1 and the rest:** merge native-metal as usual.
   - The workflow changed in four places: the `on.push.branches` line, the header comment, the `SMOKE_B` comment, and one new report step. Keep both sides on conflict.
   - New demos and bursts need nothing: they are picked up by name.
8. **Results are `.jpg` and `.mp4` now.** Shots are `<demo>-<s>s.jpg` and `other-…-0s.jpg`; each burst is `burst-<name>.mp4`.
   - Get frames with `ffmpeg -i burst-<name>.mp4 -start_number 0 burst-<name>-%02d.png`, or one frame with `-vf "select=eq(n\,7)" -vframes 1`, then Read them.
   - The gates still measure the originals.
9. **Where native-metal's results are.** A fast-forward of native-metal starts no run. The results for that sha are in your own branch's folder for the same sha, which is the run you waited for. If the fast-forward was rejected and you merged again, wait for the merge commit's run before fast-forwarding.
10. **Owner strips and clips:** extract the frames from the MP4 first. The MP4 itself opens in a browser or QuickTime and steps frame by frame.
