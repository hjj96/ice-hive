## Summary
At phone size the Stage 1 overhead (the pipes, cables and trays the bugs walk on and drop from) read as dark silhouettes over
the void: the frost crust on the routes' tops came out the void's own colour, (17, 36, 70) against (17, 33, 64) on a CI
shot, so only the black undersides read. V1 makes the layer itself read without making the frame brighter. It is a look-only
change in the overhead's own shader and geometry: no core change, no kit or baked-asset change, no new route or crossing,
+0 draw calls.

- **Where the layer has to read, the crust is lighter, its top line catches the sky and its far edge takes a cool rim.**
  A per-fragment read weight decides where (`Level.metal` `overheadReadWeight`):
  - 0 inside the cutaway's ghost (a marine's cut, a drop ring, the near-eye fade), so R3's look stays there;
  - 0.7 (the "near share") just outside a marine's cut;
  - 1 from 2.4 cut radii away: the far chunks most.
- **The three terms** (on frosted materials only):
  - the crust's albedo goes (0.46, 0.5, 0.56) → (0.70, 0.76, 0.84);
  - the topmost line (N.y 0.80–0.97) catches the cold sky, so a pale line shows with or without a lamp;
  - the far-upper edge of every frosted top gets a cool rim against the void.
  - The deck walkway grates, the duct body and the upper truss carry no frost flag. They stay dark on purpose.
- **Small cold running lights sit on the long racks over the void.**
  - They are 16 × 10 × 7 cm, ice cyan (0x8fe6ff), emission 3.0.
  - Each sits at every (k + ½) × 3.5 m of the station's x or z axis, so a rack made of 2–9 m edges keeps one rhythm. They go
    only where the lamp itself hangs over the void, never over walkable deck.
  - A soft pulse runs along the rack every 3.2 s.
  - Each is 10 triangles inside the same chunks: Mk II has 20, the showcase 5.
- **`-ohRead 0`** gives R3's look for a device A/B. Since this run it lays no lamps at all (before, they were left standing as
  dark boxes).

## Handoffs → B5 / S2
How a new arena's overhead pieces get the same crust, rim and lights. This is also in README «Передача B5 / S2».

- **The crust, the sky line and the rim belong to the overhead shader, not the kit.**
  - Anything drawn by `WorldOverhead`'s chunks (the `overhead` / `overheadCut` pipelines, `overheadShade`) gets them on
    materials with the frost flag: `KitFlag.frost` in OverheadKit, `F_FROST` in tools/blender/kit.
  - The flag is already on the `oh_*` pieces' and B2's `manifold.py` materials. The shader puts the crust only on up-facing
    faces and the rim on their far edge.
  - A piece without the flag stays a dark silhouette.
  - Geometry drawn by another pipeline (the station's `fs_level`, a boss pass) gets none of it. B5 should build the
    Manifold's trunks and overhead routes into `OverheadBuild` chunks. The cutaway, the near-eye fade, the read weight and
    the `1+1` draws then come free, with no kit change.
- **The read weight measures from the squad's cut points**, which come first in the block (`misc.y` is their count; the
  first 4 are read). Add drop-warning points and boss parts after them, or the near share starts measuring from the boss.
- **Running lights:** `OverheadBuild.runningLights` is private. The rack builder and `runOn` call it.
  - It lights runs along x or z, at (k + ½) × 3.5 m of the station axis, over the void only. Diagonal runs get none.
  - A new route kind built in `OverheadBuild` calls it the same way.
  - A lamp is a face of the `light(…)` material (layer `Trim.none` = 255, no trim uv) with uv0 = (station-axis coordinate
    in m, 1). The shader recognises it by uv0.y = 1, runs the pulse along uv0.x and scales its emission by `misc.w`. It
    goes out inside the cut.
- **S2 (the shaft):**
  - The shaft wall is not overhead, and its segments move (per-draw offsets) while overhead chunks are static.
  - To make its trays and pipes read the same way, port the three `overheadShade` terms (constants `WorldOverhead.read`)
    into the wall shader. Use weight 1 away from the squad and 0 in its cut.
  - Use the same uv0.y = 1 lamp mark only if the wall reuses `overheadShade` whole.
  - The four lift cables are vertical, so there are no tops to crust.
- **Checks:** `-ohRead 0` for an A/B, the log line `overhead: … N running lights`, the `overhead` look scene (only its
  p95/p50 floor was re-baselined), and the squad-visibility gate (`ci/overhead_vis.py`).

## Open issues
1. **No device measurement.** The ≤ 0.02 ms (iPhone 12) cost is an estimate.
2. **The lamps are small on a still frame.** At phone size a 16 cm lamp is about 3 × 2 pt, or 4–5 pt with its glow (12–15
   device pixels on the CI frames). That is clear in motion (the pulse) and in 100 % crops, but only a small dot on a still.
   If the owner wants the racks to read more, the cheap levers are `OverheadBuild.lightSize` (e.g. 24 cm) or the pulse
   amplitude in `overheadShade`, both constants.
3. **Before / after shots are different moments.** CI is not frame-deterministic: the app's simulation runs on wall-clock
   dt. The overhead scene's p95/p50 follows the moment (2.82–3.52 on unchanged code).
4. **The punch-in medians on V1's runs sit high.** The analysis above says it is the moment, not the overhead; worth a look
   on the next packages' runs.
5. **GitHub's macOS capacity.** Three of V1's runs each lost one job to «The job was not acquired by Runner of type hosted
   even after multiple attempts»: d0da734 (build), 79357be (core-tests) and b4b9481 (part b). A host-only failure needs a
   real change to re-run.
6. **The parallax burst's capture has host gaps** (two of 0.37 / 0.47 s on run 4) while the full-size JPEGs are written.
   The clip keeps the real timing.
