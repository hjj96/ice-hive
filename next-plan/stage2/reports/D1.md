## Summary
Dig In is an endless holdout at the bottom of Kelvin-9: the squad rides the lift down and holds its deck wave after wave
until it falls. It is built in IceHiveCore as a mission template (`MissionTemplate.digIn`, start path `-mission dig-in`). It
has its own calibration preset (`holdout`, calib-holdout.jsonl), gate rows and CI step, plus report lines, catalog keys, a CI
demo, a burst and a report fixture.

- **The template** (DigIn.swift): Kelvin-9 Mk II as the classic mission has it (R5's overhead routes, R6's props), with the
  squad set down on the lift's deck (`DigInStation`), so the hold starts on the first tick. The plan is one HOLD at the lift
  with time ∞, the lift's pulse, and `HoldStep.waves` (`HoldoutWaves`). It is a HOLD variant, not a new module: every other
  hold runs exactly as before.
- **The waves** (GameHoldout.swift): lull 9 s (trickle × 0.15) → build-up 6 s → peak. At the peak the wave's horde comes
  through `startHorde` in the hold posture: two packs over the lift's rim, 15 % down the overhead's routes over the zone, the
  rest from the decks on 2–4 sides, with the banner «WAVE N». The wave is held when the peak has run ≥ 8 s and the live swarm
  is ≤ max(10, 15 % of the horde), or after 40 s. Wave n's horde is 40 + 9 × (n − 1) units (× the tier's horde and
  population scale), capped at 240. Each wave held is an escalation step of the director (bug health +6 %, damage +3 %,
  specials and their caps — what a lit beacon is in the classic mission). The generic hold's horde clock and forced lull are
  off: the waves are the hordes.
- **The Queen** leads every fifth wave by her own arrival rule, `BossArrival.waves(first: 5, every: 5)` (B1's framework).
  - Her warning comes with the wave's horde (halved), and she climbs in 2 s.
  - Her wave, and its bank, is held only once she is dead.
  - Each arrival is a fresh Queen; the rider re-arms when her next wave peaks.
  - B1's time-based `.every` rule was not used: it does not know the waves, so she would come in a lull or inside another
    wave's peak.
  - If she cannot come within 40 s (no rim to climb — never on Kelvin-9), her wave is held like any other, so the hold
    cannot stall.
- **The bank:** every fifth wave held (5, 10, 15 …) banks the score: the player's kills (1 each), 250 per wave held and 1000
  per Queen. A banked score survives the fall; the rest goes with the squad. The design names waves 5 and 10; I extended it to
  every fifth wave, or nothing past wave 10 could ever be kept. The bank's banner shows the score as the report will (× the
  tier's multiplier), and the squad patches up (the tier's beacon breather).
- **HUD** (the core builds the lines): «HOLD THE LIFT · WAVE 4», «HOLD WAVE 5 TO BANK THE SCORE», «WAVE 4 HELD · REGROUP»,
  «KILL THE QUEEN TO BANK THE SCORE». The objective bar and the lift's deck ring show the way to the next bank. For a hold
  without a countdown the app had divided ∞ by ∞ (a NaN in the shader); it now uses `Game.holdProgress`. The pulse hint
  shows for the first 8 s. H1's hold banner rules apply for free (stage `.hold`).
- **Report and profile:**
  - title «HELD 12 WAVES»; a strip of the bank waves 5 · 10 · 15 · 20 · 25 · 30, lit once held;
  - «BANKED» in place of «SCORE»; lines «Your kills», «Waves held N», «Queens killed N», «Lost in the fall −N» (a part can now
    be negative); the total is the bank × the tier's multiplier;
  - the note is «best N waves» (`Profile.dig`, best waves held per tier, appended after the classes in the profile JSON) or
    the «NEW BEST» chip.
  - A Dig In run is never won: no medal, no «Try Recruit» chip and no «longer than last time» (its run leaves `lastT` and
    `lossEarly` alone). Service XP and the class's best score work as usual.
- **Tips** (catalog): the first-sighting tip `toast.tip.bank` comes at the first Queen wave. The report tip is «Banked at
  wave N — the fall couldn't take it» (green) or «Nothing banked — hold to wave 5: …» (red), beside the usual loss cause.

## Acceptance
1. **Core tests: met.** `DigInTests` has 10 tests and takes about 20 s in CI's debug build. All 284 core tests pass in CI
   (274 before, plus DigInTests' 10).
   - **The long run:** a squad that cannot fall holds out for 8 min on Veteran. The waves keep being held (none waits more
     than 150 s), nothing goes non-finite, the HUD's numbers stay finite, and the mission never ends by itself.
   - **Only the fall ends it:** on Nightmare with nobody at the controls, the squad falls and the mission is lost, never won.
   - **Wave escalation:** each wave's horde is bigger than the last; every wave held is an escalation step (bug health
     climbs); the lull and its line follow each wave.
   - **Banking:** the bank is the kills + five waves + the Queen. After the fall the score equals the bank exactly, and the
     report and profile follow it. If nothing was banked, the score is 0.
   - **The Queen's arrival rule:** waves 5 and 10 are hers, waves 1–4 and 6–9 are not. Her wave waits for her death, wave 10
     brings a fresh Queen at full health, and the hold never locks.
   - **Determinism:** two identical runs match.
   - **Allocation-free steady state:** by construction, as in B1 and B4. `HoldoutState` holds plain scalars and
     `holdoutTick` builds nothing per tick. Texts and queue entries are made only at a wave's peak or a bank, like a classic
     horde. There is no allocation-count test.
2. **Dig In rows in their bands: met.** Run 43551406432's holdout gate: Recruit 23 in 17–29, Veteran 12 in 9–15, Nightmare 4
   in 3–6. AFK first player down: Veteran 16–169 s (≤ 200), Recruit 74–150 s (≤ 270).

   - A change that moves the runs a whole wall leaves its band, and the md5 catches any change at all. The cross-seed run

   - What it means for players: Veteran banks twice in two runs of three, in about 7 minutes (the classic mission's length).

## Handoffs → S1 (Shaft 9: the ride)
- **Director code I touched.** All of it is off for any plan without `HoldStep.waves`, so the classic mission and every preset
  keep their bytes:
  - GameMission.swift: `resetMission` resets `holdout`; `beginStep` gives a holdout's Queen her own banners; `holdTick` hands
    an active hold with `waves` to `holdoutTick`; `startHold` calls `holdoutBegin`; the objective samples.
  - GameBoss.swift: `BossArrival.waves`, and the three rider functions `riderExpected`, `riderDue` and `riderRearm`. The
    `.every` path is unchanged.
  - GameHUD.swift: the objective line, its key and its bar; the pulse hint for a hold without a countdown; `runSummary.holdout`.
  - GameDebug.swift: `debugSkip` inside a holdout holds the current wave (and kills a live Queen).
  - Calibration.swift: `templateRuns` (`MissionCalib.maxs`, `tag`), the `holdout` preset, the `dig` row record, the waves bands.
  - Mission.swift: `HoldStep.waves`, `CalibBand.wavesLo/Hi`, `MissionCalib.maxs`.
- **A RIDE module** is a new `MissionModule` case plus `MissionStep.ride(RideStep)`. Every switch over the steps will ask for
  it:
  - GameMission: `beginStep`, `enterPhase`, `missionTick`, `missionZone`, `squadZone`, `calibStage`, `planStrip`;
  - GameHUD: the objective line, the marker, the hints;
  - `debugSkip`, `Game.objectivePoint` and `GameBoss.stepBringsBoss`.

  Dig In did not add a module: it is a HOLD variant. That trick fits a RIDE only if the moving deck holds like a zone. The
  ride's rhythm (a landing is the peak, the open ride the build-up) can reuse GameHoldout's phases (lull → build → peak → held)
  and `startHorde` in the hold posture (its share over the rim, its 15 % down the routes over `arena()`). H1 noted that the
  ride's moving deck is not `.hold`, so add its shape to `HUD.keep` if banners should avoid it.
- **Sector chaining:** `Game.level` is a `let`, and the flow fields, the hash, the walk grid, the overhead and the props all
  belong to the level. Swapping the level is a new Game operation, not a plan step. Dig In plays one station.
- **Preset naming** goes by family: `missions` (R7), `bosses` (B4), `holdout` (D1). Going Down needs its own name (for example
  `descents`):
  - set `MissionCalib(preset:)`; the default `missions` would change calib-missions.jsonl;
  - add the name to `Calibration.presetNames` and to the `case "missions", "bosses", "holdout"` line of `Calibration.preset`;
  - give it its own CI step;
  - set `MissionCalib.maxs` if its runs last longer than 600 s.
