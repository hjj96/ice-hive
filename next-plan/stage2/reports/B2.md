# B2 — The Threader: creature and arena kit (`native-vx-threader-asset`)

## Summary
The second Kelvin-9 boss, THE THREADER, and the Manifold arena's kit pieces now exist as assets of the headless Blender pipeline. They are baked into the game's formats, gated, reproducible and ready for B5 to draw. This package is assets only, with no IceHiveCore or App/Sources change, so calibration is byte-identical by construction (CI md5s below).

- **The creature**: three rigid IHV1 v2 files (bone texture, glow table, sockets). `IHVLoader` reads them unchanged.
  - `Creatures/threader_head.ihv` (429 KB): an armoured wedge-shaped skull under a serrated, frost-rimmed shield, with spiked cheek plates and three pale small eyes (ocelli) on each side. **Split mandibles**: each forks into two tines and opens sideways, never a round toothed mouth. A swept **crest** of three frost plates sits over a pale weak-point membrane; the crest and its sheath lift off the membrane while the head is down. The **lantern organ** is the two antennae fused into one ringed whip. It rises from the brow, arches over the jaws and hangs a pearl bulb in a cage of dark ribs. The head is drawn ×1.35 about its neck, because B4's head circle is 1.1 m against the segment's 0.75. LOD 3998 / 614 / 314. 12 clips.
  - `Creatures/threader_seg.ihv` (142 KB): ONE frost-plated segment, drawn 18 times on the chords of the body path. It has:
    - a sculpted back plate (tergite) with a keel, side keels, flanges and a flared serrated rear rim with a rime band. This pale band marks every segment at phone size.
    - two hooked spines, flange spurs and dim breathing holes (spiracles).
    - **one pair of long hooked legs** whose claws wrap under a 0.45 m trunk pipe.
    LOD 1696 / 292 / 90. 7 clips. Two of them, grip-crawl and deck crawl, are locomotion loops driven by distance travelled, with the claws planted.
  - `Creatures/threader_node.ihv` (139 KB): a lantern node in its segment's model space. A pearl sac (SAC + gland: lit from inside, with five dark veins) sits under two hinged frost shutters. States: plated and open (both lit), a pop into a torn dark husk, and dark. It replays the segment's shudder and death. LOD 696 / 176 / 72. 8 clips.
  - It is an original bug from the Runner's family: dark wet plum shell (chitin), pale rims, pink-white hive pores, here with frost-crusted plates and pearl light. It has a centipede-like body with one leg pair per segment, a beetle's armoured head with stag-like forked jaws, and a lure made from the insect's own antennae. There is no worm head, no round mouth, nothing modelled on another game's creature, and no class colour on it (pearl, mauve, frost grey).
- **The boss gate**, raised on purpose and only for bosses (tech §5.2; `tools/blender/ihv.py` `BOSSES`, `gates.py`):
  - LOD0 ≤ 8000, LOD1 ≤ 1200, LOD2 ≤ 400 triangles.
  - Each boss file ≤ 1 MiB, and all of one boss's files together ≤ 1 MiB.
  - The whole body as drawn at LOD0 (each file × the number of times it is drawn) stays within the boss's own limit. For the Threader the limit is 50 000 and it uses 1 × 3998 + 18 × 1696 + 5 × 696 = 38 006 triangles in 694 KB.
  - The rigid-path error is ≤ 1 mm per metre of the part's rest radius. 16-bit rest positions and the bone texture round a 2–3 m part by 1–2 mm. Results: head 2.29 mm against a limit of 3.49, segment 1.16 against 1.58, node 0.98 against 1.23.
  - Every other creature keeps the old budget: 3000 / 450 / 150 triangles, 384 KiB per file, and the bugs' total ≤ 1.5 MB. Boss files do not count against that total.
  - The numbers are in both READMEs.

  - `creatures/build.py` accepts a module's own triangle target (`TRI_TARGET`).

## Where design.md and B4 differ: B4 wins and the art follows it
- **The lantern.** B4's `ThreaderView.lantern` is the head point − 0.9 m. It is a view value only; nothing in the simulation reads it. Up on a trunk that point lies inside the 0.9 m pipe, and in the down window it is under the deck. The art hangs the bulb from the arching whip instead. The `lantern` socket is the beam's origin for B5: it sits 3.09 m ahead of the head point and 1.15 m above it at rest, and moves with the clips and the look yaw. The lit spot on the deck stays B4's.
- **The nodes.** B4's hit circles sit 0.55 m under the path, which is inside the trunk up there. The art seats the sac on the segment's back, drawn with the segment's transform. It has the same plan position, so aimed-up fire and the aim assist find it where it is drawn in plan.
- **The coil** is B4's: radius 3.5 → 1.2 at 2 m, not design's «7 m ring». The segment's `coil` clip braces its legs 2 m down.
- **Sizes nobody fixed**: the head ×1.35 (to match B4's 1.1 m head circle); the trunk pipe r 0.45 (the claws are built round it); the ring duct uses R3's section; the pump roof machinery rises to 3.24 m, above B4's 2.6 m cover box.
