**What I did after the reset.** The previous agent's last run, 43472168024 on 76fbeed, failed one Verify line in part b.
- **The failure:** `queen-cineoff` logged «2.02 s of sim and 2.10 s of wall», and the check allowed at most 2.09 s of wall.
- **Not the host, not the intro.** With `-cine off` the intro's time scale is 1 (`dilation x1.00 for 0.00 s`).
  - The extra 0.08 s are the game's own hit-stops inside the window. A brute killed near the player costs 0.085 s at
    ×0.12; a blast nearby costs 0.05 s at ×0.3.
  - Those hit-stops were there before this package and vary between runs: 508d41a's window had none and read 2.02 / 2.02.
  - The tick clock can explain at most 0.017 s, since no frame in that window exceeded 17 ms.
  - So the wall-time tolerance was an error in my package's own check.
- **The fix, d42e2a6:** the arrival line now splits the wall time:
  `(the intro's slowdown X s, the game's hit-stops Y s, the tick clock ±Z s)`.
  - GameScene passes its own world scale (hit-stop × a demo's freeze) to `Cinematics.update` as `worldScale`. It is only
    counted, never applied.
  - Verify now requires exactly «the intro's slowdown 0.00 s» with `-cine off`, which is what «`-cine off` restores
    today's timing exactly» means.
  - With cameras on, Verify requires the intro's slowdown to be at least 0.05 s before the arrival.
  - README «Заставка босса» is updated to match.
