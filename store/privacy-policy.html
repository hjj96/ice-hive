This policy covers the iPhone game ICE HIVE, version 2.0 and later.

ICE HIVE does not collect, upload or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. What it remembers — your settings, your profile and your records — stays on your iPhone.

  • Data not collected
  • No account
  • No analytics
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • Works offline

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites. It shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to track.

What the game stores on your iPhone

So that the game remembers your choices and progress, it keeps the following on your device, in the app's own storage (iOS UserDefaults). The storage key is given for each item.

Settings

  • the class you picked last — ledulei.cls;
  • sound on or off — ledulei.muted, and the volume level — ledulei.vol;
  • haptics (vibration) on or off — ledulei.haptics;
  • graphics quality — ledulei.quality;
  • brightness — ledulei.bright;
  • the difficulty you picked on the title screen — ledulei.diff;
  • other options you change in the game's settings, stored the same way.

Progress

  • your best result: the most kills in one mission and your fastest extraction time — ledulei.best;
  • your profile — ledulei.profile: the number of missions and extractions, the total score earned, and for each class its service points (which give its rank), missions, extractions, best score, fastest extraction, the badges earned on each difficulty, the length of its last mission and a count of early losses (used to suggest an easier difficulty); plus a counter of how often that suggestion was shown.

Tutorial and hints

  • whether you have seen the first-mission tutorial — ledulei.tut;
  • how many seconds (up to six) you have used the thumb sticks, so the on-screen stick hints can fade out — ledulei.sticks;
  • the station number for your next mission, only when the game builds a new station layout for you — ledulei.seed.

If you played an earlier version of the game on this iPhone, the new version copies your old settings and record on first launch, on the device, so an update keeps them.

None of this contains personal information, and none of it leaves your device through the game. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on in iOS, these items are included in your own backups like the data of any other app; Apple handles those backups under your iOS settings.

Network, analytics and third parties

  • The game works fully offline. All code, 3D models, sounds and fonts are inside the app; it downloads nothing.
  • There are no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools in the app.
  • There are no in-app purchases, no sign-in and no account.
  • There is no chat, no contact with other players and no user-generated content.

Data Apple may provide

If you chose in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your iOS settings. We use it only to find and fix problems, never to identify you.

If you write to us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. We never share it.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. To erase everything the game has stored, delete the app from your iPhone.
